# Product Vision

Build an independent, global, enterprise legal operations platform for corporate legal departments. It must work without Wakeely Pro and may optionally connect to it later.

Core journey:
Business request → legal request → triage → matter → contract/document → review → approvals → manual signature record/upload signed copy → repository → obligations/deadlines → audit/reporting.

Differentiators:
- Independent product and data ownership.
- Cloud, Private Cloud, and initial On-Premise deployment.
- Country, language, currency, timezone and terminology configuration.
- API-first integration.
- Arabic RTL and English LTR at launch.
- Strong tenant isolation, versioning, audit, retention and legal hold.

Positioning:
A legal department operating system, not a template download site and not a mandatory Wakeely Pro module.
