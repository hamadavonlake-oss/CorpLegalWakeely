# Seed Data Plan

Create safe non-production seed data:
- Demo organization.
- Jordan and generic country configuration.
- Arabic and English locales.
- JOD, USD, EUR currencies.
- Demo entities and departments.
- Demo roles and permissions.
- Sample request, matter, contract and document metadata.
- Sample approval rules.
- Sample templates marked DEMO ONLY.

Never seed real personal data, legal advice or real court filings.
